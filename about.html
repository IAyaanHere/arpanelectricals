Who We Are

ESTABLISHED IN 2002 • TECHNICAL INTEGRITY AT CORE

Established in 2002, Arpan Electricals & Enterprises is amongst the leading contractors and service providers of electrical substation services, transformer overhauling up to 315 MVA, on-site oil filtration, electrical panels, and turnkey electrification works.

Under the visionary leadership of our Proprietor, Mr. Nitin Kalbende, and General Manager, Mr. Amol Kalbende, our ethical work practices and technical rigor have helped us build a prestigious client base including marquee names like M/s Shapoorji Pallonji, M/s Punj Lloyd, M/s Reliance Industries Ltd, BHEL, and Central Railway.

Our permanent warehousing and workshop facility in Nagpur is fully equipped with advanced calibration and testing machinery, high-vacuum mobile oil filtration units, and an extensive stock of transformer accessories and HT/LT switchgears to respond swiftly to planned overhauls and emergency shutdowns across the country.
