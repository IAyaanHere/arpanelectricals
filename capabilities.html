03 / On-Site Oil Treatment

HIGH VACUUM TRANSFORMER & INDUSTRIAL OIL FILTRATION

Moisture, gas, and particulate contamination severely degrade transformer dielectric strength. We deploy high-vacuum dehydration, degassing, and filtration mobile plants on site to restore oil breakdown voltage (BDV) to peak operational standards.

•

Transformer Oil Dehydration & BDV Enhancement

Multi-stage heating, ultra-high vacuum chambers, and micron filtration bringing oil BDV to >70 kV and moisture content below 10 PPM.

•

Hydraulic, Turbine & Compressor Oil Filtration

Centrifugal and coalescing filtration for steam turbine oil, TG sets, compressor fluids, and heavy industrial hydraulic systems.

•

Filtration Machinery & Storage Tanks on Rent

Mobile filtration trailers, high-capacity vacuum pumps, and specialized transformer oil storage tanks available on rent for major plant outages.

04 / Cabling & Termination

HT & LT CABLE LAYING, JOINTING & TESTING

Proper cable trenching, laying, and termination are critical to preventing underground faults and water seepage. Our certified jointers strictly follow engineering guidelines and rigorous safety protocols.

•

Precision Trenching & Protective Layering

Straight trenches with gradual depth gradient (min 45cm width, 75cm depth), bottom free from stones, 9cm dry sand cushion below and above cables, protective brick covering, and 10cm tapered rain hump.

•

HT & LT Cable Jointing Kits & Terminations

Supply and expert installation of heat-shrink and push-on terminations for 1.1kV, 11kV, 22kV, and 33kV XLPE/armoured power cables.

•

Comprehensive Field Testing (Megger > 50 MΩ)

Insulation resistance testing, continuity resistance, sheathing continuity, earth testing, and high-voltage Hi-Pot testing conducted before and after jointing in the owner's presence.
